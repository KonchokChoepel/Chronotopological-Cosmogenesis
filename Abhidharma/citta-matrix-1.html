The structural zero

Akusala is absent from all supramundane and jhāna realms. Unwholesomeness cannot arise in concentrated or liberated consciousness — a topological constraint, not a moral gloss.

Kiriya absence

Kiriya (functional) is absent from Lokuttara. Path and fruition exhaust themselves — they produce no ongoing functional residue. This is why arahants have no future rebirth.

89 vs 121

The 121 count subdivides each of the 5 rūpa and 4 arūpa jhānas by the five jhāna factors, giving 40 supramundane cittas instead of 8. Same structure, finer resolution.

Vipāka asymmetry

Kāmāvacara has 23 vipāka — far more than any other realm. The sensuous sphere is where kamma ripens most densely. Higher realms have only their own jhāna results.
