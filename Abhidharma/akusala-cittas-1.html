Kāmāvacara · Akusala · 12 Cittas

The Akusala Consciousnesses

Classified by root (mūla) · feeling (vedanā) · spontaneity (saṅkhāra)

Root: lobha · dosa · moha Vedanā: somanassa · domanassa · upekkhā Saṅkhāra: asaṅkhārika · sasaṅkhārika Diṭṭhi: present · absent
Lobha-mūla Greed-rooted 8 cittas
# Citta Vedanā Saṅkhāra Diṭṭhi Character
1 Somanassa-sahagataṃ diṭṭhigata-sampayuttaṃ asaṅkhārikaṃ Pleasant Unprompted Diṭṭhi Joyful, spontaneous, with wrong view — the purest expression of lobha
2 Somanassa-sahagataṃ diṭṭhigata-sampayuttaṃ sasaṅkhārikaṃ Pleasant Prompted Diṭṭhi Same but requires instigation — weaker momentum
3 Somanassa-sahagataṃ diṭṭhigata-vippayuttaṃ asaṅkhārikaṃ Pleasant Unprompted — Joyful greed without wrong view — conceit (māna) often operative here
4 Somanassa-sahagataṃ diṭṭhigata-vippayuttaṃ sasaṅkhārikaṃ Pleasant Prompted — Prompted joyful greed without wrong view
5 Upekkhā-sahagataṃ diṭṭhigata-sampayuttaṃ asaṅkhārikaṃ Neutral Unprompted Diṭṭhi Cool, indifferent greed with wrong view — acquisitive without excitement
6 Upekkhā-sahagataṃ diṭṭhigata-sampayuttaṃ sasaṅkhārikaṃ Neutral Prompted Diṭṭhi Prompted neutral greed with wrong view
7 Upekkhā-sahagataṃ diṭṭhigata-vippayuttaṃ asaṅkhārikaṃ Neutral Unprompted — Neutral greed without wrong view — habitual attachment, pride
8 Upekkhā-sahagataṃ diṭṭhigata-vippayuttaṃ sasaṅkhārikaṃ Neutral Prompted — Weakest lobha-rooted citta — sluggish neutral greed, prompted
Dosa-mūla Aversion-rooted 2 cittas
# Citta Vedanā Saṅkhāra Diṭṭhi Character
9 Domanassa-sahagataṃ paṭigha-sampayuttaṃ asaṅkhārikaṃ Unpleasant Unprompted — Spontaneous aversion — anger, fear, grief arising without instigation
10 Domanassa-sahagataṃ paṭigha-sampayuttaṃ sasaṅkhārikaṃ Unpleasant Prompted — Aversion requiring instigation — resentment that needs to be worked up
Moha-mūla Delusion-rooted 2 cittas
# Citta Vedanā Saṅkhāra Diṭṭhi Character
11 Upekkhā-sahagataṃ vicikicchā-sampayuttaṃ Neutral — — Pure delusion with doubt (vicikicchā) — paralysis, indecision, inability to commit
12 Upekkhā-sahagataṃ uddhacca-sampayuttaṃ Neutral — — Pure delusion with restlessness (uddhacca) — distraction, agitation without object

Root determines feeling

Lobha → pleasant or neutral only. Dosa → unpleasant only. Moha → neutral only. Feeling tone is not an independent variable — it is entailed by the root.

Diṭṭhi is lobha-only

Wrong view (diṭṭhi) only co-arises with lobha. Aversion and delusion cannot carry wrong view. The system models cognitive distortion as structurally parasitic on desire.

Moha has no spontaneity axis

The two moha cittas are not classified as prompted or unprompted. Pure delusion is structurally prior to the prompted/unprompted distinction — it underlies all 12.

Dosa has no view variant

Aversion never pairs with wrong view. You cannot have doctrinally-grounded hatred in this system — hatred is always phenomenologically raw, never intellectually justified at the citta level.
